# Figure style: example (invented paper on field-free switching of Mn3Sn)

Venue: two-column physics journal that asks for lettering at least 2 mm high. Widths 86 mm and 178 mm.
Axis labels and panel labels 9 pt; tick labels, legends and notes 8 pt (capitals of 8-pt Helvetica are 2.0 mm).
Panel labels: (a), (b), ... roman, above the top-left corner of each panel.
Schematics: parallel projection, azimuth -58 degrees, elevation 24 degrees, light from the upper left.
