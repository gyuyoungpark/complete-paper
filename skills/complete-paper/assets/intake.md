# Intake: what only the authors can supply

Copy this file to `paper-state/intake.md` in the project and fill in what you know. Leave the rest blank.

- The skill never fills these fields itself. A blank field becomes a TODO marker in the manuscript.
- The one exception: a field marked "(default)" was copied from your saved defaults (`~/.complete-paper/defaults.md`, form in `assets/defaults.md`). Confirm or edit it, and the mark goes. When you fill in a field that will repeat in later papers, the skill offers to save it there.
- Write "none" explicitly where that is the answer (for example, competing interests). "None" and blank mean different things.
- When something changes, edit this file. It overrides anything said earlier in a session.
